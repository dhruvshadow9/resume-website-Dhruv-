DHRUVSINGH

Finance, strategy and service: the three threads that run through my story.

I

Roots in Udhampur

I hail from Udhampur in Jammu & Kashmir, where I completed my schooling at Army Public School and secured 80% in Class XII. Alongside my studies, the National Cadet Corps instilled in me a lasting sense of discipline, and I was awarded its “A” Certificate.

II

Scholarship in two arenas

Today I am a first-year student of the Bachelor of Business Administration in Digital Business and Entrepreneurship at the Indian Institute of Management Bangalore. In parallel, I am reading for a B.Com (Honours) at Dayal Singh College, University of Delhi, and I am a CA aspirant, charting my course towards Chartered Accountancy.

III

Service and the open trail

I lead an animal welfare club in my area society, which has extended rescue, medication and care to more than thirty animals, and I am a member of the Animal Welfare Society at Dayal Singh College. Beyond service, I have completed an 11 km trek at Shiv Ghatti, taught children in a voluntary capacity and taken part in the IIM Bangalore Case Competition.

IV

The written word

Poetry, English calligraphy, music composition and songwriting nourish the quieter side of my character. Faiz Ahmed Faiz, Ahmed Faraz, Parveen Shakir and Zia Mohyeddin are among the voices I return to most.

V

The road ahead

I aspire to attain true mastery of finance and management, to gain substantive professional experience, and ultimately to pursue advanced study in management, with financial analysis, business strategy, consulting, digital marketing and data analytics as the disciplines that draw me most.
